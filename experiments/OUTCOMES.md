# Outcomes and deviations

What happened to each hypothesis in [`PREREGISTRATION.md`](PREREGISTRATION.md), and
every change made to that plan. Confirmatory figures are on held-out items 100–149
and come from `discovery/results/CONFIRM_REPORT.md` (generated by
`discovery/confirm_report.py`) and `RESULTS_LEDGER.md`. Where the wording here and
the paper differ, the paper is authoritative.

Terminology: the pre-registration calls the per-head normalised score the *gaze*
score; the paper calls it *selectivity*.

## Deviations from the plan

1. **H4 clause 2 replaced before the confirmatory run (2026-08-17).** On exploratory
   data the low- and high-mass halves of the head set were not equivalent, so the
   equivalence prediction was replaced by a directional re-test and labelled as one.
2. **Depth-matched mass split added (2026-08-17b).** Implemented as an exact
   within-layer split and run in all three models, after every pre-registered cell.
3. **Confirmatory suite restarted after a bug.** The steering runner judged held-out
   answers against the transcripts of items 0–49. It was caught after the first
   cells, the two affected runs were discarded (they are not in this repository),
   and the suite was rerun. No exploratory number was affected.
4. **Random-control reporting rule.** Two seeds per random-K cell were fixed in
   advance. After a large spread appeared between seeds, every random figure is
   reported with its per-seed values; no seeds were added to pre-registered cells.
5. **H8/H9 held-out runs.** Promised when H8 and H9 were registered, initially left
   out of the run queue, then added and run on items 100–149.
6. **H5's interpretation narrowed.** Concentration measured during generation does
   track steering, so the claim is restricted to concentration measured at the
   prompt token, as registered.
7. **H12's text model corrected before its run.** Qwen2-7B does not share Qwen2-Audio's
   attention layout; Qwen1.5-7B-Chat does, and is a same-family relative rather than
   the model's starting checkpoint.
8. **Bunny re-measured.** Bunny numbers produced on transformers 5.x were void (the
   model computed wrongly); every reported Bunny number is from transformers 4.37.
9. **Analyses added after the plan**, reported as exploratory or secondary: the
   forced-choice re-scoring, the layer-matched null for head-set overlap, the subset
   sufficiency tests, ten layer-matched random draws on Ultravox, norm-weighted
   scores, the bias dose-response, the vision arms and layout transfer, and the
   rerouting cost of the intervention.
10. **Claims withdrawn during the work:**
    - that the published score steers below a matched random control (not supported
      across ten draws, which were also drawn at a different depth; ledger §11, §26);
    - that its failure is monotone in modality mass (Spearman ρ = −0.600 across four
      models; ledger §3);
    - that inheritance is specific to frozen backbones (H12);
    - that the unshared heads on Ultravox interfere with steering (inside their
      control distribution across five draws; ledger §19).
